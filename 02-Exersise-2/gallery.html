Self-Portrait by Frida Kahlo

Frida Kahlo, the queen of unapologetic
self-expression, takes the stage with
“Self-Portrait with Cropped Hair” (1940)
. Kahlo, known for her bold style
and unibrow, painted this self-portrait
after divorcing Diego Rivera.
In playful defiance of gender norms
, she rocks a suit and cropped hair,
making a bold statement about identity
and independence
